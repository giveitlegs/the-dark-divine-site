About

Possessed by aesthetics

raised by the internet

I'm a millennial lifestyle creator and marketing muse with more than 12 years of content creation and brand partnerships behind me. I split my time between New York, Georgia and Florida, and I make content for brands with taste and a little nerve.

The Dark Divine is my alter ego: cute enough to stop the scroll, sharp enough to say no to a brief that reads like a quarterly report. I only post about products I'd actually use. My time is for hire; my opinion isn't.
